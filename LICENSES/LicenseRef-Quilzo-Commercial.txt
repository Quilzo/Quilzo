Quilzo Commercial Licence
=========================

This file is a pointer, not a licence. It exists so that the SPDX identifier
`LicenseRef-Quilzo-Commercial`, which appears in every source file in this
repository, resolves to something a reader and a licence scanner can find.

The commercial licence is a negotiated agreement between the copyright holder
and one named licensee. It is not a public licence and there is no version of
it to accept by downloading, using, or continuing past a notice. Nobody holds
it by accident. If you have not signed one, you do not have one, and the terms
that apply to you are the AGPL-3.0-or-later text in this directory.

That is deliberate. A public "commercial licence" file would have to be a
take-it-or-leave-it EULA, and the clauses that matter in one of those --
warranty, indemnity, liability caps, termination, what happens when something
goes wrong -- are precisely the clauses that have to be negotiated to mean
anything. Publishing a template here would create the appearance of terms
without the substance of them.


What it grants

  The same software, without the AGPL's conditions. In particular, without
  the section 13 obligation to offer source to users interacting with a
  modified version over a network.

  It does not grant more software. There is no feature held back for it, no
  separate enterprise build, and no code path that checks which licence you
  hold. See LICENSING.md, which states that as a commitment rather than a
  description of the present moment.


Who needs one

  Anyone who cannot comply with AGPL-3.0-or-later, or is not permitted to.
  In practice that is: organisations whose policy prohibits AGPL code
  outright, and vendors embedding this in a product they do not intend to
  release under the AGPL.

  Everybody else already has a licence, at no cost, with no registration and
  no conversation: the AGPL one.


How to obtain one

  https://github.com/Quilzo/Quilzo -- open an issue, or use the contact in
  SECURITY.md. Expect a conversation and a signed document, not a checkout.
