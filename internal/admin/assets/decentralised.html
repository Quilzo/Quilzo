Putting it there

  1. Download the bundle below and expand it.
  2. Run ipfs add -r --cid-version=1 site/, or drop the folder into any pinning service.
  3. Check that what you get back is the identifier above. If it differs, something changed your bytes on the way.
  4. Point a name at it: set your ENS contenthash to ipfs://{{.Root}}, and readers reach it at yourname.eth.limo.

A single file's identifier is the hash of its bytes and nothing else — no name, no timestamp, no metadata. Two people with the same file always get the same answer, which is what makes any of this checkable.

What this does and does not promise

It promises that we are not hosting your site and cannot remove it, and that the address is the content rather than a pointer somebody controls.

It does not promise that every reader can always reach it. Readers arrive through gateways, gateways run on ordinary DNS, and in 2026 the main one was hijacked through its registrar, seized by a previous registrar, and blocked by a large ISP. The content survived all three; the path to it did not. Publish to a conventional host as well — two addresses, one hash.
