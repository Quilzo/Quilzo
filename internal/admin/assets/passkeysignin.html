Why this is safer than the token

A token is a string, so anybody who sees it is signed in — a screen share, a shell history, a pasted support ticket. A passkey never leaves your device, and your browser will not offer it to any site but this one. That last part is what makes it unphishable: a convincing copy of this page at another address cannot ask your device for it.
