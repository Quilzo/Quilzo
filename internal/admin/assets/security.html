The rest of the picture

This screen is the posture scan. These answer the other questions, and each is a separate check rather than a section of this one:

  • Code scan templates and content, for injection and leaked secrets
  • Content policy the Content-Security-Policy this content implies
  • Controls how Quilzo implements each NIST control, who does the rest, and an OSCAL component definition
  • Organisation policy your NIST parameters, declared and enforced, changed by two administrators
  • Security profile which features this installation runs, and the signed manifest that is CM-7 evidence
  • Site report what the law asks of the published site — accessibility, forms, personal data, AI disclosure — and a draft accessibility statement
  • Inventory the bill of materials and every algorithm in use
  • Integrity every object re-hashed, and the anchors that witness it
  • Agent activity what agents have done, as opposed to what they may do
