What no policy changes

  • Whoever raised or drafted a decision does not approve it.
  • No agent approves anything, an agent's work included.
  • Each person counts once.
  • A team of one approves alone only with the decision marked so, and a review by somebody else due within {{.Policy.ReviewDays}} days; and not for a day after another administrator stopped being one.

Signing in

Declare the policy


Each rule names a kind of record, the permission that makes somebody eligible, the level (3, or 4 for two people), the thresholds above which a second approver is needed, and how many days the approval counts. A policy that asks for fewer people than the rules above allow is refused. It applies from the moment it is declared, and the audit log records it.
