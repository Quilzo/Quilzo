What no policy changes

  • Whoever raised or drafted a decision does not approve it.
  • No agent approves anything, an agent's work included.
  • Each person counts once.
  • A team of one approves alone only with the decision marked so, and a review by somebody else due within {{.Policy.ReviewDays}} days; and not for a day after another administrator stopped being one.

Signing in

{{if .Policy.PhishingResistant}}Whoever may approve, accept a risk, publish outside the organisation or administer signs in with a passkey, or a single sign-on whose provider said it used a method that cannot be phished; anybody else is asked for their passkey before they go on.{{else}}The policy does not ask for a sign-in that cannot be phished; every approval is still a fresh passkey signature.{{end}}

A request that has gathered some of its approvals waits {{.Policy.WaitDays}} days for the rest, and then lapses with them.

The policy is declared at the store's console: quilzo approval policy shows it, and quilzo approval policy set FILE declares it.
