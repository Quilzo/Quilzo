What visitors can do

Under each answer, a visitor can choose to send their question to somebody here. It arrives in the Inbox and is kept, which nothing else a visitor asks is. They are told so, and for how long, before they send.

Counted from the last message. Up to 90 days. Leave empty for 30.

A microphone button, offered only where the visitor's browser recognises speech on their own device, and a button that reads the answer aloud with the device's own voices. Nothing they say leaves their device; what arrives here is the question as text.

Where the browser can translate on the device, a question in another language is translated into the site's, and the answer back, marked as a translation with the original a click away. The answer is still checked against your pages.
